THE PART EVERY CREATIVE KNOWS

It always started the same way.

A DM at 1 AM — "bro, free this weekend? paid work".

No contract. No advance. Just trust and a thumbs-up emoji.

I'd shoot all day. Carry my own gear. Edit till 3 AM.

Then the four worst words in this industry:

"payment next week, bro."

Next week became a month. The month became left-on-read.

The anatomy of a ghosted gig

Four steps. I lived this loop so many times I could storyboard it.

01

Found in the DMs

Scouting happened in Instagram requests and WhatsApp groups. No profile, no history, no reviews — just vibes.

02

The verbal "guarantee"

"Money right after delivery, bhai, guaranteed." The only proof it ever happened: a screenshot of the chat.

03

The shoot goes great

A 14-hour day on my feet, my camera, my cards, my patience. Everyone hugs. It feels like a win.

04

Delivery → silence

Files sent at 2:47 AM. Typing bubble appears. Then nothing. You were never a client's priority — you were an experiment.

THE TURNING POINT

The last straw wasn't even a big one.

2 AM. I was three hours into editing a stranger's wedding — the third free revision, because "the vibe wasn't premium enough." My phone lit up. Same client. New message.

"can you also do the teaser? and can we lower the price a little"

I didn't get angry. I remember laughing, actually. Because the problem was never that one client. The problem was that nothing in this industry made honesty the easy option — not for them, not for me. Verbal promises, scattered chats, no paper trail. We were all just hoping.

So I stopped hoping and started building. If the industry would never hand me a contract, I'd build the platform that does.

What we refuse to compromise on

📄 Every hire is written down

The giver uploads a contract PDF stating parties, scope, date, amount and the 50/50 rule. The taker reads it and accepts before any money moves.

🔒 Money waits for work

The full amount is deposited into escrow up front. Half is released when the shoot wraps, half when the delivery is approved — or after 5 days automatically.

⚖️ Disputes have a referee

If something goes wrong, the remaining money freezes and an admin reads the contract and the evidence: release, refund, or split.

⭐ Reputations compound

Both sides rate each other after every gig and rental. Reliability shows on your profile — and low ratings get reviewed.

How it works, in one breath

Post a gig → creators apply → you accept one and upload the contract → the full amount goes into escrow → the shoot happens → 50% releases → the deliverable is approved → the last 50% releases → everyone rates everyone. Gear rental follows the same skeleton, with inspections and a refundable deposit.
