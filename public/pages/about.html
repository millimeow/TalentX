ABOUT STUDIOX

The creative industry has trust issues. We're the receipt.

Photographers get ghosted after the shoot. Models wait weeks for payment. Gear gets rented over WhatsApp with nothing in writing. StudioX exists so that none of that is normal — every hire gets a contract, every rupee goes through escrow, and every gig ends with a rating.

📄 Every hire is written down

The giver uploads a contract PDF stating parties, scope, date, amount and the 50/50 rule. The taker reads it and accepts before any money moves.

🔒 Money waits for work

The full amount is deposited into escrow up front. Half is released when the shoot wraps, half when the delivery is approved — or after 5 days automatically.

⚖️ Disputes have a referee

If something goes wrong, the remaining money freezes and an admin reads the contract and the evidence: release, refund, or split.

⭐ Reputations compound

Both sides rate each other after every gig and rental. Reliability shows on your profile — and low ratings get reviewed.

How it works, in one breath

Post a gig → creators apply → you accept one and upload the contract → you deposit the full amount into escrow → the shoot happens → 50% releases → the deliverable is approved → the last 50% releases → everyone rates everyone. Gear rental follows the same skeleton with inspections and a refundable deposit.

Student MVP

StudioX is a course-built marketplace MVP: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL with Prisma and a vanilla frontend. Payments are simulated — the escrow logic is real, the rupees aren't. Built as a team project across six phases, from schema to deployment.
