Questions, answered

Is escrow real money?

It's a demo wallet. The deposit, 50% release, refunds and dispute splits all move real rows in a real database through real transactions — but the rupees are play money. The UI says "Demo payments" everywhere.

What counts as a "post"?

Publishing a gig on the board. Editing an existing gig is free. Applications count separately — 3 per month on Free.

Do limits reset?

Yes, on the 1st of each month. The counter is per calendar month, not a rolling 30 days.

Can I cancel PRO?

Any time, from the dashboard — you drop back to Free and keep every contract, rating and rupee.
